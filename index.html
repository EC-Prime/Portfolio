About Me

JUNIOR FULL-STACK

I started coding for real in 2025, my first year properly writing lines that weren't just tinkering with basic HTML pages a few years earlier. Since then it's been a steady climb through HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with React and TypeScript next on the list. I'm a developer who likes building things end to end, the kind of systems that actually solve a problem for someone rather than just look good in a demo. And I'm still learning, on purpose, because claiming otherwise at this stage would be more embarrassing than useful.

Outside of code: football obsessive, video game enthusiast, and a firm believer that a good philosophy book and a bad self-help podcast can both teach you something if you're paying attention. TV shows too, yes the cliché, no I'm not sorry. All of it eventually spills into content creation, because apparently having an opinion about the offside rule, a game mechanic, or a Stoic quote isn't complete until it's been turned into something to share. This site is where the coding side of that finally gets its own stage.
